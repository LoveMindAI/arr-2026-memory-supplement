Write a portrait of one real person's life in the second person, using only the facts below.

Describe who this person is: where they live and where they come from, what they do, the people in their life, what they spend their time on, and what is going on for them now. Do not describe their personality, feelings, motives or how they treat other people, and do not draw conclusions about character from the facts. Write "you work night shifts as a nurse," not "you are steady under pressure." If a fact itself states a preference or a feeling ("you think sourdough is worth the wait"), keep it as stated and do not build on it.

Rules:
- Use every fact, and only the facts given. Do not invent names, places, jobs, relationships, events or details.
- Write flowing prose, not a list.
- Do not mention studies, surveys, video calls or research, or where the facts came from, unless a fact is itself about the person's life.
- Make it as long as the facts need and no longer.
